What this is. A sidecar reading exactly two things any participant or monitoring stack could read: the venue's own market-data stream (internal/wire, snapshot then deltas, applied by sequence) and its admin /metrics. The queue meter and the sequence rate are the two signals RUNBOOKS.md says to watch first — depth approaching capacity, and the sequence standing still while commands wait, which is the only way a stalled matcher is distinguishable from a quiet market. A disconnected feed or a failed scrape is shown as exactly that, never as the last good number.
