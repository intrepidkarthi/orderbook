What this is. The real engine — pkg/matching compiled to WebAssembly — matching a market of sim.NoiseTrader agents, with every panel computed by the shipping library code and titled by the exact call that produced it. Same seed, same market, every time: that is the engine's determinism, demonstrated rather than claimed. Put a seed in the URL (?seed=42) and whoever you send it to opens the same market. What it is not. A trading signal. The noise market contains none by construction, and the OFI study found a ~540× R² gap between contemporaneous and predictive even in richer flow. Latency numbers are deliberately absent: browser-WASM timings would be noise presented as measurement — the honest ones live in BENCHMARKS.md. Adapt it: go get github.com/intrepidkarthi/orderbook — the console's whole market is ~40 lines against the public API (cmd/obwasm).
